For demo accounts only. Sign in to Roomink Work on your iPhone first and turn on receiving for the demo store. Use the same credentials below to send an incoming test call to your own device.

The call arrives immediately. Answer on your iPhone to hear a test message. The call ends automatically. This does not dial an external phone number.

Limit: one call per minute, up to 20 calls per 24 hours for the demo store.
